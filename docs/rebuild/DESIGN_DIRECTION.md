# Design direction: Software with intent

## Headline exploration

1. “Software with intent.” — useful as the brand concept; less specific as a personal introduction.
2. “I build things that work. Then I make them better.” — human and accurate, but long at mobile widths.
3. **“Make it work. Make it better.”** — selected: two short, readable lines, matched to a first-person introduction and real project evidence.

## Identity

Graphite, muted sage, and warm paper. An intentionally quiet hero gives the real portrait and the engineering direction space. The tone is a student who can explain the work and its boundaries. There are no senior-role claims, skill percentages, fake activity metrics, generated faces, or meaningless terminal decoration.

Contrast is tested on the rendered page with axe, not assumed from the palette. Typography uses the existing locally licensed Manrope with an italic Georgia accent. Main text remains separate from tiny decorative labels; all meaningful explanations have normal body-sized equivalents.

## Page composition / wireframe

1. **Graphite introduction:** clearly named student, concise headline and web-development direction, authentic portrait, résumé/projects, GitHub/LinkedIn, and existing internship intent.
2. **Paper work chapter:** one substantial AgentShield architecture explorer; a differently arranged SecondYouth real-interface capture; a command/output ImgFlow comparison. Each has a full case-study route. No wall of identical cards.
3. **Preserved workbench:** ScopeLedger and NeuroInsight have visible boundaries; eight more experiments stay in the native expandable archive.
4. **How I build:** seven short, optionally expandable steps, with evidence-based project examples rather than an assertion of a perfect process.
5. **Sage About chapter:** actual education, learning direction, motivation, and résumé link. No restored Python/AI/Go skill groups.
6. **Learning records:** all seven original certificates visible and linked, with training/simulation labels and the user-requested importance order.
7. **Restrained closing:** direct email, social links, persistent motion option, and return to the top.

## Motion

Short, consistent transform/opacity entrances; no preloader, pointer takeover, infinite decoration, parallax, or scroll hijacking. Architecture steps respond immediately to keyboard or pointer input. The comparison is a real slider over two actual CLI artifacts. Native details reveal workflow and archive content. System reduced motion and the explicit reduction control suppress optional motion. Browser-native anchor behavior and history are preserved.

## Responsive behavior

A 1,240px content grid with generous desktop gutters narrows at tablet sizes and becomes one column on mobile. The portrait keeps a natural crop without distortion. The mobile menu is keyboard-operable and has a no-JavaScript fallback. Process steps become concise vertical disclosures. Case-study navigation becomes an inline list instead of a sticky sidebar. Certificate links retain generous tap targets.

Visual review must use actual browser screenshots at desktop/tablet/mobile sizes, then revise weak layouts before final handoff.
